# Recipe Buddy kitchen

Recipe Buddy is a private collection of recipes shared between chefs who know one another. Chef levels recognize both the size of a collection and feedback earned from other chefs.

## Language

**Chef**:
A person with a Recipe Buddy identity who saves, cooks, shares and reviews recipes.
_Avoid_: User, member, customer in product copy

**Chef name**:
The chosen name shown on recipes, contributions and reviews. It may differ from the email used to sign in and connect with friends.
_Avoid_: Username, handle in product copy

**Friend**:
A chef with an accepted connection to another chef. A connection permits explicit recipe sharing; it does not share a collection automatically.

**Apron review**:
A chef's editable 1–5 apron rating, with an optional note, on a recipe shared with them. The recipe's own chef cannot review it.
_Avoid_: Stars, likes, separate apron level

**Chef points**:
The combined score from currently saved recipes and apron ratings received on those recipes. Each recipe contributes ten points and each received apron contributes two points.
_Avoid_: A recipe-only rank, separate reputation currency

**Chef level**:
One of seven named ranks determined by chef points: Toast Rookie, Whisk Whisperer, Pan Wrangler, Sauce Sorcerer, Flavor Alchemist, Feast Maestro, and Apron Legend.

**Kitchen twist**:
A contributed variation of a recipe that leaves the original ingredients and method unchanged.

**Recipe conversation**:
Questions and discussion about a recipe or kitchen twist. Comments are distinct from scored apron reviews.
